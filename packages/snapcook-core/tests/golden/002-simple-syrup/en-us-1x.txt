Simple syrup
============================================================
Equal-ish parts sugar and water — a one-step recipe that still needs mass, volume and a range.

Serves 1 · en / us

Ingredients
-----------
  - 7 oz sugar
  - 0.8–1 cup water

Steps
-----
  1. Warm 0.8–1 cup water and stir in 7 oz sugar until fully dissolved and clear.
