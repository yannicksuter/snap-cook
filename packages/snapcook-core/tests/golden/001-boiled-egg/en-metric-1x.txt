Soft-boiled egg
============================================================
The simplest recipe there is: proof that a linear list is just a degenerate graph.

Serves 2 · en / metric

Ingredients
-----------
  - 1 l water
  - salt a pinch
  - 2 egg

Steps
-----
  1. Bring 1 l water with salt a pinch to a rolling boil.
  2. Lower 2 egg into the boiling water and simmer 6 minutes for a soft yolk.
