Rösti
============================================================
The Swiss potato classic — the milestone's acceptance recipe, authored in German and read in both languages.

Serves 4 · scaled 2x · en / us

Ingredients
-----------
  - 3 1/2 lb potato
  - 2 medium onion
  - salt to taste
  - pepper to taste (optional)
  - 2 3/4 oz butter

Steps
-----
  1. Coarsely grate 3 1/2 lb potato and 2 medium onion, then season with salt to taste and pepper to taste.
  2. Melt 2 3/4 oz butter in the frying pan at 355 °F, add the grated potatoes, press flat and fry for 15 min until the underside is golden, then flip.
