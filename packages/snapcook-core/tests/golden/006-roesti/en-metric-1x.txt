Rösti
============================================================
The Swiss potato classic — the milestone's acceptance recipe, authored in German and read in both languages.

Serves 2 · en / metric

Ingredients
-----------
  - 800 g potato
  - 1 medium onion
  - salt to taste
  - pepper to taste (optional)
  - 40 g butter

Steps
-----
  1. Coarsely grate 800 g potato and 1 medium onion, then season with salt to taste and pepper to taste.
  2. Melt 40 g butter in the frying pan at 180 °C, add the grated potatoes, press flat and fry for 15 min until the underside is golden, then flip.
