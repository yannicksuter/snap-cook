Herb omelette
============================================================
Approximations, a pan, a timer and a temperature in one small dish.

Serves 1 · en / metric

Ingredients
-----------
  - 3 egg
  - salt to taste
  - pepper to taste
  - 15 g butter

Steps
-----
  1. Beat 3 egg with salt to taste and pepper to taste.
  2. Melt 15 g butter in the non-stick pan at 160 °C, pour in the beaten eggs and cook for 3 min.
