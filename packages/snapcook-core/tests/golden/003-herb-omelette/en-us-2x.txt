Herb omelette
============================================================
Approximations, a pan, a timer and a temperature in one small dish.

Serves 2 · scaled 2x · en / us

Ingredients
-----------
  - 6 egg
  - salt to taste
  - pepper to taste
  - 1 oz butter

Steps
-----
  1. Beat 6 egg with salt to taste and pepper to taste.
  2. Melt 1 oz butter in the non-stick pan at 320 °F, pour in the beaten eggs and cook for 3 min.

Notes
-----
  ! not scaled automatically (manual); check this value for a 2x batch
