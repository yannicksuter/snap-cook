Pancakes
============================================================
A named intermediate — the batter — and a butter that must not scale with the batch.

Serves 4 · en / us

Ingredients
-----------
  - 7 oz flour
  - 1 1/4 cup milk
  - 2 egg
  - 1 tbsp sugar
  - 0.7 oz butter

Steps
-----
  1. Whisk 7 oz flour, 1 1/4 cup milk, 2 egg and 1 tbsp sugar into a smooth batter and rest it 15 minutes.
  2. Fry ladles of the batter in 0.7 oz butter in a frying pan at 355 °F until golden on both sides.
