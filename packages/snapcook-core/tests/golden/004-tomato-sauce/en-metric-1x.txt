Tomato sauce
============================================================
Two states prepared apart, then joined — the first genuinely non-linear recipe.

Serves 4 · en / metric

Ingredients
-----------
  - 30 ml olive oil
  - 1 medium onion
  - 2 garlic
  - 6 tomato
  - salt to taste

Steps
-----
  1. Warm 30 ml olive oil and soften 1 medium onion and 2 garlic without colouring.
  2. Crush 6 tomato by hand.
  3. Combine the softened aromatics with the crushed tomatoes, season with salt to taste and simmer for 30 min.
